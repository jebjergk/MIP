# Trust, maturity, and qualification

**Maturity** summarizes how much historical evidence supports relying on a pattern family for downstream decisions. **Trust gates** express whether that evidence meets policy for production-style workflows.

Neither maturity nor trust is a market forecast. Low sample sizes, regime change, and symbol-local divergence can all keep a pattern in research-only posture even when recent paper results look attractive.

Use **Training Status** and **Structural Training Intelligence** together: the grid gives tabular evidence; structural views emphasize setup families and filters relevant to proposals.
